# Design Explanation & Trade-off Prompt

Generate grounded design explanation for the customer's updated bathroom suite.

GROUNDING REQUIREMENTS:
- Reference actual catalog facts (e.g. Numi 2.0 dual-flush 3.8 LPF, Purist WaterSense 4.5 LPM).
- State total price and remaining budget headroom explicitly.
- Quantify water savings where data supports it.
- Explicitly disclose trade-offs (e.g. "Upgrading to cast iron bath increases luxury score to 95 but reduces budget headroom by ₹1,48,000").
- State assumptions and spatial clearances.
