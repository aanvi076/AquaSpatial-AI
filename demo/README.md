# KOHLER AI Bathroom Designer — Demonstration Guide

This directory contains resources, user journey walkthroughs, and recommended demonstration scenarios for evaluating the **KOHLER AI Bathroom Designer & Planner**.

---

## Recommended Demonstration Scenarios

### Scenario 1: The Luxury Master Bathroom Transformation
1. **Design Brief**: Input dimensions `12 x 9 ft`, Budget `₹4,50,000`, Style `Modern Luxury`.
2. **AI Suite Synthesis**: Inspect the **Luxury Suite** featuring the *Numi 2.0 Smart Toilet*, *DTV Digital Shower Interface*, and *Artifacts Freestanding Cast Iron Bathtub*.
3. **3D WebGL Showroom**: Switch camera views (*Isometric*, *Top Plan*, *Front*), test the *Vibrant Brushed Moderne Brass* finish switcher, and toggle *Katalyst Rain Shower Spray*.
4. **Specification Export**: Open the **Specification & BOM Modal** and click **Print / Save PDF** to generate the official procurement sheet.

### Scenario 2: Conversational AI Copilot & Real-Time Modification
1. Open the floating **AI Copilot** drawer.
2. Prompt: *"Upgrade to a luxury smart toilet but keep total budget under ₹3,50,000."*
3. Verify deterministic recalculation: The Copilot swaps the toilet, locks user-specified fixtures, recalculates physical clearances, and presents a grounded trade-off summary with 0 clearance violations.

### Scenario 3: Multimodal Floor Plan & Photo Ingestion
1. Navigate to the **Multimodal Vision** tab.
2. Select a sample bathroom floor plan or architectural layout image.
3. Review extracted room boundaries, detected plumbing walls, and recognized fixture locations with confidence percentages.
4. Click **Apply Dimensions to Studio** to instantly transfer detected constraints to the 2D layout and 3D showroom.

### Scenario 4: Section 14 What-If Sensitivity Matrix
1. Navigate to the **What-If & Sustainability** tab.
2. Select *"What happens if I remove the bathtub?"*.
3. Review quantified metrics:
   - Budget savings: ₹85,000+
   - Free floor area gain: 14.5 sq.ft
   - Expanded circulation corridor and enhanced dry-zone clearance.
