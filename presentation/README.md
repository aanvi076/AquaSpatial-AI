# Competition Presentation & Pitch Deck Structure

This directory outlines the key presentation narrative for the **KOHLER AI Bathroom Designer & Planner**.

---

## Slide-by-Slide Narrative

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Title**: KOHLER AI Bathroom Designer & Planner
- **Tagline**: Where Multimodal Generative AI Meets Deterministic Architectural Engineering.
- **Presenter**: Aanvi Bindal

### Slide 2: The Problem
- Bathroom renovations are complex, expensive, and error-prone.
- Generic AI models (ChatGPT, Midjourney) produce visually pleasing concepts that are physically impossible (ignoring plumbing lines, door swings, and NKBA clearance standards) and hallucinatory (fake model numbers and wrong prices).

### Slide 3: Our Solution — The Hybrid Engine
- **Generative AI** understands user intent, extracts constraints from natural language and photos, and expands design exploration.
- **Deterministic Engineering Engine** rigorously enforces boundary geometry, OBB collision detection, water-efficiency calculations, and exact GST financial math.

### Slide 4: Key Capabilities & Differentiators
1. **131 Verified Kohler Products**: Grounded catalog intelligence with authentic prices in INR, physical dimensions, flow rates, and verified source links.
2. **2D & 3D Interactive Showroom**: Synchronized floor plans and real-time WebGL 3D visualization with Three.js parametric meshes.
3. **Conversational Copilot**: Context-aware redesign agent that preserves locked fixtures and explains trade-offs.
4. **Sustainability Intelligence**: Annual water savings in liters/gallons, INR utility savings, and carbon reduction metrics.
5. **Section 14 What-If Matrix**: Instant sensitivity analysis for spend deltas, fixture removals, and value engineering.
6. **Procurement & BOM PDF Generation**: Ready-to-build specification schedules with 18% GST and rough-in allowances.

### Slide 5: Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, Lucide Icons.
- **Backend**: FastAPI, Pydantic v2, Uvicorn, Python 3.12.
- **AI/ML**: Hybrid TF-IDF/Cosine RAG Retrieval, Multimodal Computer Vision, Prompt Safety Policies.
- **Quality Assurance**: 87/87 Automated Tests Passing (100%), 0 TypeScript Build Errors.
